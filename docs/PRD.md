# Aegistra — Product Requirements Document

## Product thesis
Small and mid-sized organizations are adopting AI faster than they are building governance processes. Aegistra is a lightweight system of record for AI use: what exists, why it exists, who owns it, what needs review, and what evidence supports the decision.

## Target customer
10–250 employee B2B SaaS companies, agencies, consultancies and professional-services firms selling into US/EU customers.

Primary buyers: founder/COO, security lead, privacy/compliance lead, IT lead, or fractional CISO.

## Jobs to be done
- Show every AI system in use and its owner.
- Prioritize which systems need review first.
- Track open governance actions and due dates.
- Maintain evidence that reviews happened.
- Answer customer and audit questions faster.

## MVP
- Authentication and workspace onboarding
- Multi-tenant AI system register
- Governance-priority scoring
- Owners and review dates
- Action tracking
- Evidence-readiness view
- Internal admin surface
- Marketing, legal, SEO and analytics
- Supabase RLS

## Out of MVP
Billing, statutory legal classification, runtime prompt monitoring, shadow-AI discovery, file uploads, SSO and framework-control mapping.

## Success metrics
Activation: first workspace + AI system in <10 minutes.
Validation: 10 design partners and at least 3 willing to pay $49+/month.
Retention proxy: return visit to update a review/action in week two.

## Guardrails
Aegistra is not legal advice, certification, or an automated statutory AI-risk classifier.
