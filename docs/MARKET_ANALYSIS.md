# Market Analysis — October 2026

## Categories considered
- LLM observability and cost monitoring: strong demand, but crowded.
- SaaS renewal/spend management: recurring pain, but already well served from low-end trackers to procurement suites.
- General compliance automation: large TAM, but expensive to build credibly and highly competitive.
- AI governance/system inventory: increasingly formalized, while many smaller companies still operate from spreadsheets and policy documents.

## Selected wedge
Aegistra focuses on organizations that need an AI inventory, ownership, recurring reviews and evidence before they need a full enterprise GRC platform.

## Why now
AI governance is becoming a formal software category. Public guidance increasingly recommends maintaining an AI systems register, and customer/security questionnaires are creating practical pressure even for smaller vendors.

## Differentiation
- Self-serve rather than enterprise implementation-heavy.
- Governance metadata rather than model-traffic interception.
- Transparent prioritization rather than pretending to provide legal classification.
- Designed around ongoing ownership/review/evidence instead of a static spreadsheet.

## Commercial hypothesis
Free → Team $49/month → Business $149/month, with pricing validated through design partners before billing work is prioritized.
