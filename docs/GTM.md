# Go-to-Market

## Positioning
A living AI register for teams too small for enterprise GRC.

## ICP
US/EU B2B SaaS companies, agencies and consultancies with roughly 10–250 employees.

## Message
Know where AI is used. Know who owns it. Be ready when customers ask.

## Acquisition
1. SEO around AI register/inventory templates and small-business AI governance.
2. Free 3-system tier and downloadable register template.
3. Founder-led outreach to security, privacy and COO roles.
4. Partnerships with fractional CISOs, privacy consultants and MSPs.
5. Later: assurance-pack watermark/product-led sharing.

## Pricing hypothesis
Free: 3 systems.
Team: $49/month.
Business: $149/month.
Validate willingness to pay before adding enterprise complexity.
